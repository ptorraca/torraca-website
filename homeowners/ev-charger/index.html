What's involved in a home EV charger install?

Most homes can take a charger, but a few things need checking first. Here's what's typically involved:

  • A licensed electrician is required by law, DIY is illegal
  • Your switchboard may need capacity checked or upgraded
  • A dedicated circuit runs from the board to the charger
  • Most homes suit a 7kW single-phase charger
  • Three-phase homes can charge faster if the car supports it
  • Smart chargers can be set to run on off-peak power
