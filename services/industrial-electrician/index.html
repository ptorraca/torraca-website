When do you need an industrial electrician?

Industrial electrical work involves higher voltages, heavier loads, and more complex systems than residential or commercial work, and it carries a higher risk when not handled correctly. You'll need a licensed industrial electrician when:

  • Installing or commissioning industrial machinery
  • Upgrading to 3-phase power for heavy equipment
  • Setting up a new warehouse, factory, or manufacturing space
  • Carrying out high-bay lighting installations
  • Performing compliance testing in industrial facilities
  • Dealing with recurring faults or tripped industrial circuits
