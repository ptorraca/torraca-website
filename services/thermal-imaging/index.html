Catch electrical faults before they become dangerous.

Torraca Electrical uses professional thermal imaging to identify hidden faults, overloaded circuits and fire risks in switchboards and electrical systems, before they become a problem.

Call 0483 932 387 Get a quote

Electrical faults don't always trip a breaker or blow a fuse. Many develop silently, in switchboards, behind walls, and in connection points, generating heat that gradually becomes a serious fire risk. Thermal imaging lets us see these faults without opening walls or disturbing your systems.

Our licensed electricians use FLIR-grade thermal cameras to scan your switchboard and electrical systems, identifying hotspots, loose connections, overloaded circuits, and failing components that standard inspections can miss.

Whether you're a homeowner wanting peace of mind or a business requiring an insurance-grade report, Torraca Electrical can help. Contact us to book a thermal imaging inspection.

Why is thermal imaging important?

Electrical faults are one of the leading causes of house fires in Australia, and many of these fires could have been prevented with early detection. Thermal imaging is the most effective non-invasive way to identify electrical risks. You should consider a thermal inspection if:

  • Your switchboard is more than 15 years old
  • Your insurance requires an electrical risk assessment
  • You're buying or selling a commercial property
  • You've had repeated fuse or breaker trips
  • You manage a facility with high electrical demand
  • You want proactive peace of mind about your electrical system

A thermal inspection typically takes 1 to 2 hours and provides a detailed written report. Book yours today.
