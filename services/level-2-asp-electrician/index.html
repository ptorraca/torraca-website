Level 2 ASP Electrician for Ausgrid Connections

Torraca Electrical is an accredited Level 2 Authorised Service Provider, no. 5760. That means we can do the network-side work a standard electrician is not licensed to touch, and do it in the same visit as the rest of the job.

Call 0483 932 387 Get a quote

Most electricians stop at the meter. Anything on the network side, the service line from the pole, the consumer mains, the metering, has to be done by a Level 2 Authorised Service Provider. If your electrician is not Level 2, they have to sub it out, which adds a trade, a delay and a markup.

We hold that accreditation ourselves. So when a job needs a new connection, an upgraded service line, a metering change or a fault repaired on the incoming supply, we handle it end to end, including the paperwork with Ausgrid.

Do you need a Level 2 electrician?

Anything on the network side of the meter has to be done by a Level 2 Authorised Service Provider. You need one when:

  • You are connecting a new build or knock-down rebuild to the grid
  • Your consumer mains need upgrading to carry more load
  • A storm or age has damaged your service line
  • You are moving from single phase to three phase
  • Supply needs disconnecting and reconnecting for works
  • Your metering needs installing or altering
