What are your electrical obligations as a strata manager?

Strata managers and owners corporations have specific legal obligations when it comes to electrical safety in common areas and shared systems. These include:

  • Maintaining common area lighting and electrical systems
  • Ensuring emergency lighting is tested every 6 months
  • RCD (safety switch) testing in common area switchboards
  • Smoke alarm compliance in common areas
  • Addressing any electrical defects identified in building reports
  • Providing compliant electrical infrastructure for tenants
