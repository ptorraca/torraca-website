What is an electrical emergency?

Not sure if your situation is an emergency? If in doubt, treat it as one. Call us immediately if you experience any of the following:

  • Complete loss of power to your home or business
  • Visible sparking, arcing, or burning from any fitting
  • A burning smell with no obvious cause
  • An electric shock, however minor
  • A safety switch that won't reset
  • Storm damage involving your electrical system or meter box

While waiting for us to arrive: switch off the affected circuits at your switchboard if safe to do so, keep clear of any exposed wiring, and if you smell burning, leave the premises and call 000 first.
