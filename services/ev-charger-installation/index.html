What do you need for an EV charger installation?

Most homes can accommodate a home EV charger, but there are a few things to check before installation. Here's what's typically involved:

  • A licensed electrician is required by law, DIY is illegal
  • Your switchboard may need an upgrade to handle the additional load
  • A dedicated 32A circuit is recommended for most home chargers
  • Smart chargers can be configured to charge during off-peak tariffs
  • Solar system owners can integrate EV charging for maximum savings
  • Commercial installs may require load management and network approval
