High-performance sports lighting that lets the game go on.

Torraca Electrical designs and installs LED sports lighting for ovals, courts, fields and multi-use facilities across Hornsby and the North Shore. Built for performance, efficiency and long-term reliability.

Call 0483 932 387 Get a quote

Whether you're a local sporting club, a local council, or a multi-sport facility, the right lighting system transforms your venue, extending usable hours, improving player visibility, and reducing long-term energy costs. Torraca Electrical brings the technical expertise to design and install sports lighting systems that meet Australian Standards and your specific lux requirements.

We handle everything from the initial design and pole installation to the electrical supply, control systems, and ongoing maintenance, working around your schedule to minimise disruption to your users.

Ready to upgrade your facility's lighting? Contact us for a consultation and free design quote.

Our sports field lighting services

LED sports lighting design and supply

  • Lighting design to AS/NZS 2560 standards
  • Lux level calculations for your sport and level
  • LED fixture selection and specification
  • Council and authority submission support
  • Photometric modelling and reporting

Pole and floodlight installation

  • Sports lighting pole supply and installation
  • Foundation design and concrete works
  • LED floodlight mounting and aiming
  • Underground cabling and switchboard connection
  • Network provider approval and coordination

Controls, timing and maintenance

  • Timer and automated control system installation
  • Remote access and app-based control options
  • Coin-operated and booking system integration
  • Ongoing maintenance and lamp replacement
  • Fault response and emergency repair

What sports facilities can Torraca light?

Our sports lighting experience covers a wide range of facility types across the North Shore and Greater Sydney:

  • Cricket ovals and multi-use sports fields
  • AFL, rugby, soccer, and football grounds
  • Tennis courts and netball courts
  • Basketball courts, indoor and outdoor
  • Athletics tracks and multi-sport precincts
  • School and council sporting facilities

Each project is different, so we start with a site assessment and lux report to ensure the lighting design meets your sport's requirements. Get in touch to arrange a free consultation.
